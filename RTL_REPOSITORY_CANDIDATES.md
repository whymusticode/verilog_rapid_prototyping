# Synthesizable FPGA RTL Repository Inventory

This implementation-oriented inventory was made by inspecting shallow clones on 2026-09-03. “Usage” identifies the generator command, package manifest, build entry point, or RTL top to start from. Only synthesizable contents are summarized; repositories can also contain testbenches, formal properties, software, and documentation.

The submodules were not installed because `AGENTS.md` forbids changes to `RTL_examples/`. The 34 proposed additions remain in `proposed-rtl-examples.gitmodules`; `dblclockfft` is already installed.

## Practical filtering notes

- The LiteX repositories and VexRiscv are generators: their Python/Scala elaboration emits synthesizable Verilog.
- Corundum, BlackParrot, PULPino and many ADI projects are platforms with dependency trees; use their manifests/build systems rather than compiling every HDL file.
- The alexforencich libraries remain useful stable Verilog, while Taxi is the actively developed SystemVerilog successor.
